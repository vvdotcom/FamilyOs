Hometide

Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}. This policy covers the Hometide phone and web app, the Hometide app for Fire TV, and the Hometide Alexa skill. {{OPERATOR_SENTENCE}}

In short: Hometide keeps your family's calendar, chores, rewards, lists, meals and photos so your family can see them on your TV and phones. A parent creates the account and decides what goes in it. We use the data only to run Hometide for your household. We don't sell it, show ads, or use tracking or advertising tools. You can delete your account and all household data at any time at /delete-account.

Who uses Hometide

Only an adult parent or guardian can create a Hometide account. Children don't create accounts and don't need an email address. A parent can add children as profiles in the household and can let a child use a limited "child view" on the parent's phone or browser, or see the family screen on the TV. The parent controls and can delete everything about their children at any time.

Service providers we use

  • Amazon Web Services: Amazon Cognito holds parent sign-in details. Amazon Bedrock reads a flyer or photo you choose to import, checks a chore photo, writes the daily family briefing, writes a recipe from the short description you type, and answers voice requests. Only the information needed for that task is sent. AWS states that Bedrock does not use your inputs or outputs to train AI models.
  • Railway: hosts the Hometide service and its database.
  • U.S. National Weather Service: receives your rounded home area to return a forecast.
  • Calendar providers you connect, when we fetch your calendar link.
  • Amazon Alexa, only if you link the Hometide skill.

They process data on our behalf to provide Hometide. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Children's privacy

Hometide is designed to be set up and controlled by parents. We collect information about a child only when their parent adds it: a first name, the chores, rewards and events the parent assigns, and photos the parent or child takes of finished chores while the parent has turned on child view. Children cannot create accounts, change settings, connect calendars, or approve anything without the parent's four-digit code. We don't use children's information for advertising, profiling, or anything other than running Hometide for that family. A parent can review, change or delete any child's information in the app at any time, or delete the whole account. If you believe we hold information about a child without a parent's permission, contact us and we will delete it.

AI features

Briefings, flyer imports, chore-photo checks and voice answers are generated by AI and can be wrong. They are suggestions: a parent always confirms changes, and chore photos are approved by a parent, not by the AI.

How long we keep data

  • Household content stays until a parent deletes it or deletes the account.
  • Chore photos are deleted when a parent decides on them.
  • Phone and web sessions expire after 14 days; paired TVs after 90 days unless renewed.
  • When you delete your account, your household and everything in it are deleted from our database immediately. Copies in our hosting provider's backups expire on that provider's backup schedule.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are handled by Amazon Cognito. Calendar links are encrypted, the parent code is stored only as a salted verifier, and each household's data is kept separate and checked on every request.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change everything in your household in the Hometide app or web app.
  • Delete items, photos, connected calendars, paired TVs and voice connections at any time.
  • Sign out everywhere from Settings.
  • Delete your account and all household data: see how to delete your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your data. Contact us to use them.
