Delete your Hometide account

A parent can permanently delete their Hometide account and all of the household's data. You don't need to install anything: it works in any web browser.

Delete it yourself (takes a minute)

  1. Open the Hometide web app (or the Hometide phone app) and sign in as the parent.
  2. Go to Family → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password, and confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your parent account and sign-in.
  • Your household: family members and children's profiles, events, chores, stars and rewards, lists, meals and recipes, family photos, chore photos, connected calendar links, weather location, and the parent code.
  • All signed-in phones and browsers, child views, paired TVs and linked voice assistants are disconnected.

Deletion is immediate and can't be undone. Copies in our hosting provider's backups expire on that provider's backup schedule. We keep nothing else about your household. See the privacy policy for details.
